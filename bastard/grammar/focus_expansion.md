# Focus Expansion

Use focus expansion when a global mark needs local evidence.

Plan:

- Keep the global panel as the primary panel.
- Give the focus item the strongest contrast or halo.
- Add one subordinate detail panel only when sample-level or replicate-level data exists.
- Connect global focus to detail with a restrained connector or aligned label.
- Do not invent uncertainty or subgroup structure from compatibility columns.

Risks:

- detail panel larger than the global claim
- focus chroma used on decoration instead of data
- local samples shown without clear relationship to the global item
