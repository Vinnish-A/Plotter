# Composition

Composition combines a main data body with support panels, annotation tracks, legends, or summaries.

Rules:

- Main body first; supports attach around it.
- Support panels must justify their area by data density or interpretive value.
- Annotation tracks carry metadata, not the primary coordinate system.
- Avoid duplicate axes when panels share row or column identity.
- Keep legends close enough to decode the panel they serve.

Retinue owns rendering and output validation.
